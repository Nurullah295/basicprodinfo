Fixcef

Dosage Form, Generic & Strength: Fixcef 250/500 mg, Fixcef Plus 250/500 mg & Dry Syrup (Cefuroxime / Cefuroxime + Clavulanic Acid)

Indication

  • Pharyngitis, Tonsillitis, Acute bacterial otitis media & Maxillary sinusitis.
  • Acute bacterial exacerbations of chronic bronchitis (AECB) & Pneumonia.
  • Uncomplicated skin and skin-structure infections, UTIs, and Gonorrhea.

Highlighting Points

  • Ultimate choice for resistant pathogens: "Suicidal inhibitor" of ß-lactamase.
  • Ensures very low resistance and higher sensitivity compared to Cefuroxime alone.
  • An ideal drug for UTIs with a 97% clinical success rate.
  • Significantly improves lung function.
  • USFDA Approved pregnancy category B Drug.

Dosage & Administration

  • Bronchitis course: 5 to 7 days. Other infections: 7 to 10 days (administered without regard to meals).
  • Pharyngitis/Tonsillitis (Adults): 250mg b.i.d. for 5-10 Days.
  • Uncomplicated UTIs (Adults): 250mg b.i.d. for 7-10 Days.
  • Uncomplicated Gonorrhea (Adults): 1,000mg as a Single dose.
  • Pediatric Otitis Media & Sinusitis: 250mg b.i.d. for 10 Days.
