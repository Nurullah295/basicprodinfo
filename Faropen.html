Faropen

Dosage Form, Generic & Strength: Faropen 150 mg & 200 mg Tablet (Faropenem)

Indication

  • URTIs (Pharyngitis, Tonsillitis) & LRTIs (Acute bronchitis, Pneumonia).
  • ENT Infections (Otitis externa, Tympanitis, Sinusitis).
  • SSSIs / Skin infections (Pustular acne, Folliculitis, Subcutaneous abscess, Secondary wound infections).
  • UTIs (Pyelonephritis, Cystitis, Prostatitis) & Gynecological infections.

Highlighting Points

  • Broad-spectrum penem antibiotic with powerful efficacy.
  • Effective against a wide range of respiratory, urinary, and skin pathogens.
  • Provides a reliable option for complicated skin and soft tissue structures.

Dosage & Administration

  • General Dose: 1 Tablet 3 Times Daily for 7-14 Days.
  • URTIs & SSTIs: 150mg t.i.d. (can be increased to 200mg t.i.d.).
  • LRTIs, UTIs & ENT Infections: 200mg t.i.d. (can be increased to 300mg t.i.d.).
