Allertin

Dosage Form, Generic & Strength

Allertin 20 Tablet (Bilastine INN 20 mg) & 60ml OS (2.5mg/ml)

Indication

  • Allergic rhinitis (SAR and PAR).
  • Chronic urticaria.

Highlighting Points

  • Improves quality of life: Allertin does not cross the blood-brain barrier, so it does not produce sedation.
  • Does not hamper daily activities like office work & driving.
  • Offers successful control of allergic rhinitis.
  • Ensures quickest relief from chronic urticaria.

Dosage & Administration

  • Adults: 20 mg bilastine (1 tablet) once daily.
  • Children (2-11 years): 4 ml once daily (Allertin OS).
  • Children / Adolescents (> 12 years): 8 ml once daily (Allertin OS).
