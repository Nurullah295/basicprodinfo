Clonipres

Dosage Form, Generic & Strength: Clonidine HCL 0.1 mg

Indication

  • Hypertensive Urgencies.
  • Hot Flash.
  • ADHD (Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder).

Highlighting Points

  • Clonipres: A non-hormonal treatment of hot flash.
  • Clonipres ER: A safer non-stimulant approach for ADHD.
  • Ensures quick onset of action.
  • Effective for the management of Hypertensive urgency.
  • Effectively control Hotflashes.
  • Significantly controls ADHD.

Dosage & Administration

  • Dose for Hot flash: Initially 0.05 mg (1/2 tablet) twice daily for 2 weeks, then increased if necessary to 0.2 mg (2 tablet) twice daily.
  • Hypertensive urgency: Initially 0.05-0.1 mg three times a day, increase dose every second or third day, usual maximum dose 1.2 mg daily.
  • ADHD (6 years to older): 0.1 mg (1 Tablet) once daily at bed time, adjusted weekly to a maximum daily dose 0.4 mg.
