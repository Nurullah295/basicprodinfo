Loxodol

Dosage Form, Generic & Strength: Loxodol 4 mg & 8 mg Tablet (Lornoxicam)

Indication

  • Pain management in Osteoarthritis, Rheumatoid arthritis, and Spondylitis.
  • Musculoskeletal pain, sciatica, and post-surgery inflammation.
  • All types of mild to moderate pain relief.

Highlighting Points

  • Reduces pain rapidly with fewer side effects.
  • Ensures faster onset of action (only 30 minutes).
  • Assures GI & Cardiac safety.
  • More effective and safer than Diclofenac.
  • Potential to be an excellent alternative to morphine and tramadol.

Dosage & Administration

  • Adult: 8-16 mg daily divided into 2-3 times (Maximum dose: 16mg/day).
  • Kidney and liver impairment: Maximum dose is 12 mg daily into 2 or 3 divided doses.
