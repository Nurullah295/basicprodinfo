Arokast

Dosage Form, Generic & Strength: Montelukast USP 10 mg (Film Coated Tablet) & 4 mg, 5 mg Flash Tablet

Indication

  • Asthma (prophylaxis and chronic treatment).
  • Allergic Rhinitis.
  • Exercise Induced Bronchoconstriction (EIB).

Highlighting Points

  • The real star in asthma management: Montelukast is the #1 prescribing anti-asthma drug worldwide.
  • Excellent for Long term use in chronic asthma & allergic rhinitis.
  • Ensures high success rate & better compliance In Children (Has no impact on children's growth).
  • Suitable in pregnancy (Category-B).

Dosage & Administration

  • Adults (15 years of age or over): 10 mg /day in the evening.
  • Children (6-15 years of age): 5 mg /day in the evening.
  • Children (6 month - 6 years of age): 4 mg / day in the evening.
