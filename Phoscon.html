Phoscon

Dosage Form, Generic & Strength: Phoscon Tablet (Ferric Citrate INN 210 mg equivalent to Ferric Iron)

Indication

  • Hyperphosphatemia in Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) on Dialysis.
  • Iron deficiency Anemia in Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) not on Dialysis.

Highlighting Points

  • Ensures dual benefits (Phosphate control + Anemia improvement).
  • Ensures better phosphate control and high patient satisfaction.
  • Ensures less elemental Intravenous (IV) iron requirement.
  • Ensures desired serum Ferritin, Hemoglobin (Hb) & Phosphate levels compared to Sevelamer.

Dosage & Administration

  • CKD on Dialysis (Hyperphosphatemia): 2 tablets orally 3 times per day with meals (Max: 12 tablets daily).
  • CKD Not on Dialysis (Iron Deficiency Anemia): 1 tablet orally 3 times per day with meals (Max: 12 tablets daily).
  • Dose can be titrated at 1-week or longer intervals.
