Neumir

Dosage Form, Generic & Strength: Neumir 2.5 mg, 5 mg & 10 mg Tablet (Mirogabalin Besylate)

Indication

  • Neuropathic Pain (NP) & Peripheral Neuropathic Pain (PNP).
  • Diabetic peripheral neuropathic pain (DPNP).
  • Chemotherapy-induced peripheral neuropathy.
  • Post-herpetic Neuralgia (PHN), Low Back Pain (LBP), and Spinal Pain.

Highlighting Points

  • Results in a better treatment outcome than immediate-release (IR) Pregabalin.
  • 17 times more potent than conventional (IR) Pregabalin.
  • 40 times more potent than Gabapentin.
  • Offers a significantly better quality of life with fewer side effects.

Dosage & Administration

  • Adult initial dose: 5 mg twice daily.
  • Gradually increase by 5 mg at weekly intervals up to 15 mg twice daily, if required.
  • Dose must be reduced to half for renally impaired patients.
