Duracef

Dosage Form, Generic & Strength: Cefixime Trihydrate (Capsule, Dry Syrup PFS/DS, Paediatric Drops & Max)

Indication

  • MDR Typhoid Fever & Enteric Fever.
  • Urinary Tract Infections (UTI) & Respiratory Tract Infections (RTI).
  • Otitis media, Tonsillitis, Bronchitis, and Gonorrhoea.

Highlighting Points

  • Third generation antibiotic with excellent gram-positive and gram-negative activity.
  • The drug of choice in MDR enteric fever and parenteral to oral switch therapy.
  • First-line antibiotic in community-acquired RTIs and highly effective in UTIs.
  • Children friendly taste and flavor (Pineapple & Orange for PFS, Banana for Paediatric Drops).
  • Convenient once daily dosing, safe & well-tolerated in adults & children.

Dosage & Administration

  • Adults (RTI, UTI, Pneumonia): 200 mg every 12 hours OR 400 mg OD for 7-14 days.
  • Gonorrhoea (Adults): A single oral dose of 400 mg.
  • Children (> 6 months): 8 mg/kg/day in 1-2 divided doses.
  • Children (6 months - 1 year): 75 mg daily.
  • Children (1 - 4 years): 100 mg daily.
  • Children (5 - 10 years): 200 mg daily.
