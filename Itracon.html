Itracon

Dosage Form, Generic & Strength: Capsule 100mg, SUBA Capsule 65mg, Tablet 200mg, Oral Solution 50mg/5ml (Itraconazole)

Indication

  • Oropharyngeal Candidiasis & Esophageal Candidiasis.
  • Tinea corporis, Tinea cruris, Tinea pedis, Tinea manuum.
  • Vulvovaginal candidiasis, Fungal keratitis, Pityriasis versicolor.
  • Onychomycosis, Histoplasmosis, Aspergillosis, Cryptococcal infection.
  • Prevention of systemic fungal infection in severely neutropenic patients.

Highlighting Points

  • Itracon: Better in efficacy than Fluconazole and 1st line drug for tinea infections.
  • The most effective drug for all types of superficial fungal infections.
  • Safe and effective for all age groups.
  • Itracon SUBA: Therapeutic levels achieved in both fed and fasted conditions.
  • SUBA delivers consistent blood levels for lower patient variability.
  • SUBA Itraconazole is safe, well-tolerated, and ensures patient satisfaction.

Dosage & Administration

  • Onychomycosis: 200 mg once daily for 3 months OR pulse therapy (200 mg twice daily for 7 days, repeat after 21 days; fingernails 2 courses, toenails 3 courses).
  • Tinea pedis / corporis / cruris / manuum / Pityriasis versicolor: 200 mg once daily for 7 days.
  • Vulvovaginal candidiasis: 200 mg twice daily for 1 day.
  • Oropharyngeal candidiasis: 100-200 mg twice daily for 2 weeks.
  • Systemic candidiasis: 200 mg every 12 hours for 2 days, then 200 mg once daily for max 12 days.
