Lopesim

Dosage Form, Generic & Strength: Lopesim Tablet (Loperamide HCl 2 mg + Simethicone 125 mg)

Indication

  • Acute diarrhea associated with gas-related abdominal discomfort including bloating, cramping, or flatulence.

Highlighting Points

  • Ensures faster relief from diarrhea & gas-related abdominal discomfort.
  • Effectively reduces the duration & symptoms of diarrhea within 48 hours.
  • Ensures patients' optimum comfort.

Dosage & Administration

  • Adults: Initial dose is 2 tablets followed by 1 tablet after each unformed stool (Max: 4 tablets/day).
  • Children (9-11 years): 1 tablet initially followed by ½ tablet after each unformed stool (Max: 3 tablets/day).
  • Children (6-8 years): 1 tablet initially followed by ½ tablet after each unformed stool (Max: 2 tablets/day).
